Start with three steps.

  1. Open TypeCat from Applications. Click its menu bar icon to open the controls.
  2. Allow Input Monitoring in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring.
  3. Open Preferences → Test keyboard connection and press five physical keys. The counter must reach 5/5 to confirm actual global input delivery.
Sounds or character reactions are not working.

Confirm TypeCat is enabled, the app is not muted, and a sound is selected. A permission toggle alone does not prove event delivery: use Preferences → Test keyboard connection and physically press five keys. The test does not read or display typed text. Secure input, such as password fields, may restrict reactions. If macOS requests authentication or relaunch, complete those steps yourself.

Can I import my own image or sound?

Yes. Use the character or sound editor in Preferences. The free release keeps one custom character and one custom sound set; a sound set can contain up to 32 imported sources with five key-category assignments. Replacing or deleting an imported copy does not change the original file.

How does the timer work?

Start a countdown or Pomodoro from the menu bar or Timer preferences. The remaining time appears below your companion. Reset the timer before changing an active mode or duration. Turning TypeCat off pauses it; resume it when ready. After relaunch, the saved remaining time can be resumed. Time does not count down while the app is closed.

How can I compare sounds or change volume?

Use the preview button to listen without changing your current sound. Save favorites and browse keyboard, effects, or instrument categories. The menu bar and Sound preferences provide app volume and mute controls. Timer alerts have a separate volume.

How do I remove my files and reset TypeCat?

Back up any images or sounds you want to keep, then quit TypeCat. For the Mac App Store edition, open Finder → Go → Go to Folder and inspect ~/Library/Containers/app.squishkey.mac/. Confirm it belongs to TypeCat before moving it to Trash. This removes the app’s local settings and imported copies. Uninstalling the app alone may leave this data behind. Older development editions may separately keep data in ~/Library/Application Support/app.squishkey.mac/ and their preference domain. Do not delete other apps’ folders.

Does TypeCat record what I type?

No. Key categories and event timing are processed locally and temporarily for sound and animation. Typed characters and sentences are not extracted, logged, saved, or transmitted. Read the privacy policy for details.

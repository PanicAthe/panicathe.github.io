Privacy policy

Your typing stays on your Mac.

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

TypeCat does not collect typed text. The app does not have accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

1. Keyboard events

With your Input Monitoring permission, TypeCat uses a passive, listen-only global event tap to temporarily process key categories and time between events for sounds and character animation. It does not extract, log, store, or transmit the characters or sentences you type. The keyboard connection test counts five physical key presses received by this same global event tap; it does not collect text.

2. Settings and imported files

Settings and copies of images and audio you choose to import are stored locally on your Mac. Original files are not modified. Images are re-encoded as PNG; imported sound copies and trimmed playback clips are kept in the local library. They are not uploaded to a TypeCat server.

3. Collection, sharing, and tracking

The app has no user account, advertising, analytics, or tracking features. It does not send app usage or personal data to a TypeCat server and does not sell or share your data with advertisers. App Store downloads, payments, and Apple-provided services are governed by Apple’s own policies.

4. Website and support

This website does not add advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies. Website hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the website. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement and GitHub Pages data collection for details.

If you contact support by email, your email provider and our support mailbox process your address and the message you send. We use those messages to respond to your inquiry. Please do not include passwords, typed text, or personal media. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

5. Retention, deletion, and permissions

You can remove or replace imported items from the character and sound editors. Local settings and files may remain after uninstalling the app. Back up anything you need and follow the complete removal steps on the support page. Revoke Input Monitoring at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring.

6. Changes and contact

TypeCat maintains this policy and updates its effective date when its data practices change. Questions about privacy or your local data can be sent using the contact information below.

Contact TypeCat

Please include your macOS version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, typed text, or personal media.

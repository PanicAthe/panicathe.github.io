Three steps
and you are set

From the permission prompt to the five-key test, the first launch walks you through it.

  1. 1

    Meet in the menu bar

    Open TypeCat, then click its small menu bar icon.

  2. 2

    Connect your keyboard

    Allow TypeCat in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring.

  3. 3

    Five taps. Ready to go.

    In Preferences → Test keyboard connection, press five physical keys to confirm delivery.

A permission toggle is not the same as input actually arriving. These are first-launch previews; the real check is five physical key presses in the app.

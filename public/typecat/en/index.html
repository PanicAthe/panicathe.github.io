SMALL THINGS, GOOD COMPANY

A softer rhythm
for your everyday.

Lives in your menu bar. A few thoughtful touches, ready when you are.

01 / SOUND

Find your sound.

Explore 12 bundled sound themes, from mechanical keys and typewriter clicks to gentle instruments. Preview, save favorites, or import your own audio.

02 / COMPANION

A small friend, nearby.

Choose Aqua Cat, Cat, Dog, or Rabbit. Your companion responds to typing, can look in different directions, and can be moved and resized. You can also use your own image.

03 / FOCUS

Make room for focus.

Use the countdown or Pomodoro timer from your menu bar. Your companion displays the remaining time. Pause, reset, and choose a separate alert sound and volume.
